Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

HA Backup is a private app used only by one household to back up its own Home Assistant installation to its own Google Drive.

What the app accesses

The app only creates, reads and deletes the backup files it creates itself in the signed-in user's Google Drive. It does not access any other files, emails, contacts or other Google account data.

What happens with the data

Backup files are sent directly from our Home Assistant device to our Google Drive. No data is sent to, stored by or shared with anyone else. Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
