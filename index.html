About

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at Accurat. I hold a PhD from Ghent University, where my research focused on Ultra-Wideband (UWB) technology, wireless communication, and localization. My doctoral work explored self-adaptation, error correction using Deep Reinforcement Learning, and the application of Transformer networks to improve indoor positioning accuracy.

Research & Publications

My PhD research focused on applying machine learning to wireless communication systems. I used Deep Reinforcement Learning and Transformer architectures to address challenges in Ultra-Wideband positioning, including methods for self-supervised ranging error correction and improving indoor positioning accuracy.
